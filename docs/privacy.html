Plink privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Plink is a puzzle game for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and how. Plink is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13.

Information that stays on your device

Plink saves your name and avatar (which you choose when you first open the app), your level progress, stars, scores, achievements, streaks and settings. It also saves the state of the hint timer (when your next hint unlocks and how many video hints you used today) and simple usage counts, such as how long your first win took, how many sessions you played per day, and how many ads were shown or watched. This information is stored only on your device. We do not run a server or an account system, and we do not receive this information.

Advertising

Plink shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, general location derived from the IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. Whether ads are personalized depends on your choices below.

Plink shows three kinds of ads. A banner ad appears at the top of the game screen. A full-screen ad may appear between levels after you have played a few. Full-screen video ads are also offered to unlock a hint sooner; these are optional, you can skip the offer and wait for the hint timer instead, and you only receive the hint if you watch the ad to the end.

You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), Plink asks for your choice about ads and data use with Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Profile, then Privacy settings, when that option is shown.
  • iPhone tracking. On iPhone, Plink asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Google settings.

Leaderboards

Plink includes a leaderboard that is stored on your device. If online game services (Apple Game Center or Google Play Games) are added in a future version, your name and score may be shared with that service under its own privacy policy.

Children

Plink is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us.

Data retention and deletion

Because your game data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled under Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
